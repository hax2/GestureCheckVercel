Methods and boundaries.

01

Pair on the item

Uniform all-1 and all-5 test runs are removed first. Each model score is then paired with the human mean for the same video and dimension.

02

Read several metrics

Correlation captures ordering; MAE and RMSE capture distance; mean bias captures systematic over- or under-rating.

03

Keep inference modest

Model values are single generated judgments. Human n varies by video, and the language distribution is uneven.
